## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the gravitas of a generational Indian export house operating at global scale. The aesthetic merges the tactile, archival opulence of Indian spice and textile trade with the razor-sharp precision demanded by international institutional buyers across North America, Europe, and the Middle East.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Established Authority:** Unwavering, time-tested reliability grounded in Visakhapatnam’s maritime trade corridor.
- **Opulent Materiality:** Rich organic depths—raw jute, hand-carded cotton, whole tellicherry peppercorns, and sun-dried turmeric translated into precise digital surfaces.
- **International Rigor:** Transparent specifications, phytosanitary certifications, container-load tracking, and crisp commercial clarity.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Editorial Heritage** with **Structured Maritime Brutalism**. The interface rejects standard flat SaaS conventions in favor of warm, tactile paper substrates, fine metallic hairline rules, and asymmetric editorial layouts that evoke archival manifest registers, engraved currency, and high-end botanical cataloging.

## Colors

The palette mirrors harvest geography and mercantile heritage: deep emerald canopy, aged harvest brass, washed shore sand, and coastal paper stocks.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Green (`#096931`):** Deep forest emerald. Represents agronomic stewardship, export viability, and institutional permanence. Used for key callouts, major button outlines, and key structural badges.
- **Secondary Gold (`#B97E2E`):** Warm metallic harvest gold. Evokes saffron threads, maritime brass instruments, and prestige certification seals. Applied to primary CTAs, hairline borders, and category eyebrow tags.
- **Dark Band Forest (`#05301A`):** Deep night forest green. Anchors high-impact contrast bands, heavy footers, maritime route maps, and executive manifest headers.
- **Background Ivory (`#FAF7F0`):** Warm premium paper ivory. The primary canvas substrate; replaces cold digital white to evoke archival rag paper and raw unbleached cotton.
- **Surface Sand (`#F1EBDD`):** Soft warm sand. Used for elevated cards, inner container wells, spec sheet drawers, and tabular data groupings.
- **Body Charcoal (`#1E2A23`):** Deep pine charcoal. Provides crisp editorial legibility without the harshness of pure black (`#000000`).
- **Muted Sage (`#5C6B62`):** Structural midtone for metadata labels, secondary tabular data, breadcrumbs, and inactive pagination ticks.
- **Green Tint (`#E8F0EA`):** Soft botanical wash utilized for status chips (e.g., "In Stock", "Phytosanitary Cleared", "FOB Active").

### Color Application Rules
- Gold rules and dividers must consistently render at 30% opacity (`rgba(185, 126, 46, 0.3)`) when set against `#FAF7F0` or `#F1EBDD` to preserve hairline subtlety.
- High-contrast sections alternate strictly between `#FAF7F0` (light trade catalog) and `#05301A` (night port logbook). Dark bands must use gold typography accents (`#B97E2E`) and ivory body copy (`#FAF7F0`).

## Typography

The type system creates deliberate tension between classical display serif authority and ultra-clean modernist geometric readability.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Styling
- **Editorial Serpents (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for display statements, commodity collection titles, and historical milestones. Set with slightly tightened tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to mimic hot-metal press title fonts. Never set Playfair Display in all-caps for running copy.
- **Commercial Precision (`DM Sans`):** Carries all technical specifications, Incoterms descriptions, commodity grades (e.g., Sona Masoori Raw 5% Broken), and transactional form elements. Line-heights remain generous (`1.6x` to `1.7x`) to facilitate effortless parsing of dense regulatory information.
- **The Eyebrow Device:** Crucial to the visual identity. All section categorizations, origin stamps ("VISAKHAPATNAM PORT / BERTH 4"), and status categories are rendered using `eyebrow-bold`: strictly uppercase, bold weight, secondary gold `#B97E2E`, and wide tracking (`0.15em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an **Asymmetric 12-Column Grid** constrained to a max-width of `1280px`. It breaks the standard SaaS mold by employing editorial column splits (e.g., 5-column storytelling paired with 7-column commodity tables, or 4-column index headers with 8-column visual frames).

### Grid & Canvas Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and a maximum canvas width of `1280px` centered with fluid outer margins (minimum `2.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `1.5rem` outer margins. Reflow 5/7 column pairs into stacked or 4/4 block alignments.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1rem` canvas margin. Visual media stacks directly above technical data blocks.

### Meridian & Frame Devices
- **Gold Hairline Dividers:** 1px borders set with `#B97E2E` at 30% opacity running between columns and dividing product specification modules.
- **Offset Picture Frames:** Product hero imagery uses a visual motif where an inner content block is offset 12px or 16px from an outer 1px hairline gold border frame, reflecting framed export charters.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry digital drop-shadows and neomorphic extrusion. Depth is architectural, flat, and tactile, achieved strictly through **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Hairline Geometry**.

### Elevation Stack
1. **Canvas Substrate (Ground):** The unyielding foundation in Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`).
2. **Surface Sand (Layer 1):** Flat containers and specification panels in `#F1EBDD`. Separated from the ground via a 1px solid hairline border in `#B97E2E` at 20% opacity. Zero box-shadow.
3. **Elevated Document/Flyout (Layer 2):** RFQ drawers, quotation worksheets, and modal inspection certificates use `#FAF7F0` backed by a razor-sharp, double-stroke outline: 1px `#B97E2E` at 40% with a single crisp ambient rim (`0 4px 20px rgba(5, 48, 26, 0.06)`).
4. **Dark Band Sections (Contrast Well):** Deep Night Forest Green (`#05301A`) provides full ground recess for shipping corridors, global route maps, and annual volume metrics.

### Tactical Textures & Meridian Accents
- Backgrounds may incorporate an ultra-faint micro-paper texture (SVG noise pattern at 1.5% opacity) to eliminate digital sterility.
- Map sections and port diagrams leverage concentric 1px gold arc strokes at 15% opacity to evoke historical navigation charts.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Tailored Sharp (Level 1)** shape geometry. 

- Base interface components (buttons, input fields, data tables, and modal edges) are constrained to a strict `2px` to `4px` corner radius.
- Standard borders are crisp and block-like, reflecting heavy freight shipping containers, customs stamps, and legal bills of lading.
- Badges and quality grade indicators use a crisp `2px` corner radius. Completely rounded or pill-shaped geometries (`rounded-full`) are strictly prohibited, as they dilute the heritage, high-value B2B seriousness of the brand.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Inquiry / RFQ):** Solid Harvest Gold (`#B97E2E`) background, crisp `#FAF7F0` text, 2px border radius. Padding: `12px 28px`. Hover: shifts to deep golden brass (`#9E6821`) with no shadow change.
- **Secondary Action (View Specifications):** Transparent fill with a crisp 1.5px border in Deep Forest Emerald (`#096931`), body text in `#096931`. Hover: `#096931` fill with `#FAF7F0` text.
- **Tertiary / Link (Document Download):** Plain text with an underline offset of 4px using a 1px gold hairline. Accompanied by a 45-degree arrow icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Unfilled or `#F1EBDD` surface tint, 1px perimeter border in `#B97E2E` at 30% opacity, 2px border radius. 
- **Focus State:** 1.5px solid border in `#096931`. No glow/focus ring; clean border color change only.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md` (`13px`, semi-bold) in `#1E2A23`. Helper text and unit indicators (e.g., "Metric Tons", "FOB Visakhapatnam") use `data-mono` in `#5C6B62`.

### Cards & Commodity Listings
- Built on `#F1EBDD` background with 1px border (`#B97E2E` at 25% opacity).
- Product photography utilizes an inset golden frame or 12px offset border.
- Cards feature an upper eyebrow badge (e.g., `SPICES • HS CODE 0904`), a `Playfair Display` title, and a structured 2-column key-value data list for harvest year, moisture percentage, and minimum order quantity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Origin / Standard Tags:** Compact, sharp-cornered (2px radius), upper-case tracked labels.
- **Phytosanitary & Organic Status:** `#E8F0EA` background, `#096931` text, 1px border in `#096931` at 20% opacity.
- **Export Grades (e.g., "Sortex 100% Clean"):** `#F1EBDD` background, `#B97E2E` text, 1px border in `#B97E2E` at 40%.

### Tables & Trade Manifests
- Dense, data-rich B2B layout. Table headers use `#FAF7F0` with 1px bottom border in solid `#B97E2E` (40% opacity), text set in `eyebrow-bold`.
- Alternating row zebra strips are forbidden; rows are delineated strictly by 1px hairline dividers in `#B97E2E` at 15% opacity.
- Hovered row transitions to a subtle wash of `#E8F0EA` at 50% opacity. Numeric and technical specs align right using `data-mono`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Square 16px x 16px boxes with a 1px border in `#096931`. Selected state fills with `#096931` featuring an ivory check mark. No rounded circles for checkboxes; radio elements use an inner square dot rather than a circle.
